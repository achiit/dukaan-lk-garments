About the Showroom

A Malsisar shop built on handshakes, not hype

I started LK Garments in 2012 with a small counter and a few racks. My uncle ran a cloth shop in the bazaar, and I grew up watching him measure every customer by eye and never rush a sale. I still remember the first shirt I sold, to a neighbour who came back the next week with his brother.

Over the years families kept asking for more, so the shop grew. Today we carry ladies suits, anarkalis and sarees on one side, and gents shirts, jeans and ethnic wear on the other, with the BK Brand range for men. The habit is still the same. You sit, you try, you ask questions. If the fit is not right, we will say so, even if it costs us a sale.

Come in for a single shirt or a full wedding wardrobe for the family. Either way, you will be treated like a guest in our home.
